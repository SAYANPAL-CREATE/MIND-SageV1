# Verified sources and asset notes

Last checked 12 November 2026. Users should verify helpline details and service availability before clinical or institutional deployment.

## Government mental-health and emergency services

- National Mental Health Programme (NMHP), District Mental Health Programme (DMHP) and Tele-MANAS: Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India. https://dghs.mohfw.gov.in/national-mental-health-programme.php
- KIRAN, Tele-MANAS and other support helplines: Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Government of India. https://depwd.gov.in/en/others-helplines/
- 1098 Child Helpline (now managed under Mission Vatsalya and district/state administration): https://www.childlineindia.org/ . The official Childline notice also directs callers to emergency response 112 when necessary.
